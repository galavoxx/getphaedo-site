Phaedo

Your cognitive fingerprint, on every AI surface.

Every AI tool today re-learns you from scratch — or never learns you at all. You repeat yourself. You re-explain context. You watch the model guess wrong about how you'd actually decide.

Phaedo learns how you think — communication style, working style, decision pattern — from your real usage, into a portable, encrypted, user-owned cognitive fingerprint built on your own device. It travels with you: any AI tool knows you from the first message, and autonomous agents can consult your decision pattern — “would my human proceed here?” — instead of guessing.

Local. Encrypted. Yours. The fingerprint never leaves your devices unencrypted, and consultations return signals — never your data.
