Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Who we are: Phaedo is made by Phaedo Labs ("Phaedo", "we"). Contact: hello@phaedo.so.

The short version

Phaedo learns how you write, work and decide, so AI assistants can answer the way you like and check with you before they act for you. That profile lives on your own devices: your phone, your browser, and your computer if you use Phaedo with Claude Code or Claude Desktop. We do not have a copy, and we cannot read one.

The one shared piece is a relay that passes sealed messages between your devices when they are not on the same Wi-Fi. It cannot open them, and it forgets them on a timer.

There is no Phaedo account, no email address, no password, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not sell data or use it to train models.

What Phaedo keeps, and where

On your phone (the Phaedo app — your main copy)

  • Your profile: your interview answers, what Phaedo has learned about how you write and decide, your rules and guardrails, examples of your writing, and decisions you've committed to. Encrypted; the key is in the iPhone Keychain, unlocked with Face ID.
  • Which browsers and apps are paired with your phone.
  • A backup, only if you make one: an encrypted file you keep, opened by a recovery code only you hold. We keep no copy and no recovery key, so we cannot restore it for you.

In your browser (the Phaedo extension)

  • A copy of your profile, in an encrypted vault whose key cannot be read out of the extension.
  • Your chats with AI sites are not stored. When you turn on learning, the extension reads the page you are already viewing, turns what it sees into counts and traits (for example, "asked for a shorter answer"), and discards the text. Nothing is uploaded.

On your computer (Phaedo for Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or another AI app)

Everything here is kept in a .phaedo-mcp folder in your home folder.

  • A copy of your profile, so assistants keep working when your phone is away. Encrypted, but the key is stored in a protected file beside it, which is weaker protection than your phone's Face ID–protected Keychain.
  • Decision records. Each time an assistant asks Phaedo before acting for you (for example a merge, a deploy, a message or a payment), Phaedo records what the action was, a short description of it (up to 500 characters), any amount, who or what it was aimed at when it went through a connected app (such as a recipient, a channel or a subject line, never the message itself), what Phaedo answered, and what you decided. Encrypted. The most recent 5,000 are kept.
  • Signing records, for spending approvals you signed with Face ID: a fingerprint of what was signed and when, never the approval itself. Encrypted. The most recent 5,000 are kept.
  • Style counts learned from your Claude Code sessions, and from the Claude app on a Mac if you install Phaedo's screen reader: how often you asked for shorter answers, no headings, and so on, per session. A plain, unencrypted file of names and numbers only — never your words. The last 200 sessions per app are kept; older ones are folded into totals.
  • Claude Code's own session files are Claude's, not Phaedo's. Phaedo reads them on your computer to learn, keeps only the counts above, and discards the text.
  • Screen text in the Mac Claude app (only if you install the reader) is read on screen, turned into counts, and discarded. It is never stored.
  • Your pairing with your phone, so this computer can reach it.

Before a merge, Phaedo's check runs your own git and gh commands to see how large the change is and whether its checks passed. gh contacts GitHub as it always does; Phaedo sends nothing of its own.

Optional local models (off unless you turn them on). You can point Phaedo at an AI model of your choosing — by default, one running on your own computer — to help it classify what it reads or judge a decision. When you do, the text being classified, or the description of the action, goes to the model address you set. That model's own terms then apply.

On the relay (shared, sealed)

The relay (phaedo-relay.fly.dev) carries messages between your phone and your other devices when they are not on the same network. It is a post box, not a filing cabinet.

A small amount of routing information rides outside each seal so the relay can deliver it: the kind of message (for example "profile"), an opaque identifier for it, and a timestamp.

Who else is involved

  • Fly.io hosts the relay. It processes the traffic that reaches it, including IP addresses.
  • Apple delivers push notifications and distributes the iPhone app. A push says only "something is waiting", never what.
  • Hugging Face hosts the optional on-device model for the browser extension. If you turn it on, your browser downloads the model from them, so they see a download request. The model then runs on your computer; nothing you read or write is sent to them.
  • GitHub receives the requests your own gh command makes during the merge check.
  • The AI assistants you use (such as Claude or ChatGPT) receive what Phaedo adds to your conversations and the answers it gives them — that is what the product is for. Their own privacy policies govern what they do with it.

We do not sell data, share it for advertising, or use it to train models.

Your choices

  • Turn injection off, and Phaedo stops adding your profile to chats.
  • Turn learning off, and it stops reading your conversations.
  • Disconnect any browser or app from your phone's Access screen; it loses access immediately.
  • Delete everything on your phone with Delete from this iPhone, which also disconnects every paired browser and app so nothing can sync it back.
  • Delete everything in your browser with Settings → Delete everything.
  • Delete everything on your computer by deleting the .phaedo-mcp folder in your home folder. If you installed Phaedo from its source folder, its uninstaller does this for you (node install.mjs --uninstall --purge). Uninstalling the Claude Code plugin or the Claude Desktop extension leaves that folder in place, so your pairing survives a reinstall; delete it to remove everything.

Because we hold no copy of your data, deleting it is something you do yourself, on your devices. The only thing on our side is the relay's short-lived routing state described above, which expires on its own. You can also write to hello@phaedo.so with any question or request.
